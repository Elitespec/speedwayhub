# Gisborne Giants Claim First Superstock Teams Title in a Decade

The **Gisborne Giants** have won the ENZED NZ Superstock Teams Championship at **Robertson Prestige International Speedway** in Palmerston North, claiming their first title in 10 years since the team reformed.

The two-night event (February 7-8) saw teams from across the country battle through qualifying and finals in one of the most anticipated events on the Speedway New Zealand calendar.

The Giants' victory is a remarkable comeback story. After reforming a decade ago, the Gisborne-based team has steadily built depth and experience, culminating in this championship triumph at RPIS.

Superstock Teams racing remains one of the most spectacular events in New Zealand speedway, with coordinated team tactics and full-contact racing creating an atmosphere unlike any other motorsport event in the country.
