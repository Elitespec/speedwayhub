# Ben Jenkins Defends Streetstock Tri-Series Crown

Dunedin-registered driver **Ben Jenkins** has successfully defended his "Lift N Shift" **Streetstock Tri-Series** title.

The series, which traveled through **Blenheim**, **Nelson**, and **Greymouth**, was a war of attrition characterized by high mechanical damage and physical racing.

Jenkins finished with **198 points**, narrowly edging out Ryan Musgrove on 195 points. The series served as a vital form-guide for drivers heading into the upcoming national championship in Nelson, proving that Jenkins is in peak form when it counts.
