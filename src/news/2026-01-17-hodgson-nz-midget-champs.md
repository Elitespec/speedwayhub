# Aaron Hodgson Charges to Glory in 83rd NZ Midget Championship

**Aaron Hodgson (35M)** has won the 83rd New Zealand Midget Championship at **Baypark Speedway** in Tauranga, charging from the third row to take the chequered flag by 1.3 seconds.

The two-night event (January 16-17) saw a quality field of Midget racers battle it out on Baypark's clay surface. Teenage sensation **Alec Insley** set the pace as top qualifier, but it was Hodgson who proved strongest when it mattered most.

Veteran **Brad Mosen (10A)** finished second in what was his sixth runner-up finish at the NZ Midget Championship — a record of consistency that is both impressive and heartbreaking. **Ben Morrison (97A)** completed the podium in third.

Six-time champion **Michael Pickens** led early laps but ultimately finished sixth, while young gun Insley's qualifying pace couldn't quite translate to the final, finishing fifth.
