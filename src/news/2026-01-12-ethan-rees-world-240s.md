# Ethan Rees Triumphs in Thrilling World 240s Father-Son Battle

The 39th running of the **TWS World Invitation Superstock Championship** at Rotorua’s **Paradise Valley Speedway** ended in dramatic fashion, with **Ethan Rees (127G)** taking the title.

In a high-attrition final heat, Ethan narrowly defeated his father, **Peter Rees (10G)**, by a single point to claim the championship.

International star **Frankie Wainman Jr. (515GB)** finished third on the podium, adding international flair to one of the most prestigious individual events on the New Zealand calendar. The collision of generations and international talent made for an unforgettable night of racing in the sulphurous city.
