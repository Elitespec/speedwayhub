As of March 2026, the 2025/2026 New Zealand speedway season has delivered unforgettable moments. Here are all the major championship results.

## New Zealand Championship Results

**NZ Sprintcar Championship** (Central Motor Speedway, Cromwell, Jan 10): Daniel Thomas (2NZ) led all 25 laps in a dominant display. Joey Myers Jr (3NZ) 2nd, Kaleb Montgomery (95US) 3rd.

**NZ Midget Championship** (Baypark Speedway, Jan 16-17): Aaron Hodgson (35M) charged from the 3rd row to win the 83rd championship by 1.3 seconds. Brad Mosen (10A) 2nd, Ben Morrison (97A) 3rd.

**NZ Superstock Championship** (Wellington Speedway, Jan 17-18): Brad Uhlenberg from Oceanview Speedway (Whanganui) won the coveted 1NZ title.

**NZ Sidecar Championship** (Kihikihi Speedway, Feb 1): Aaron Rose & Nigel Sturgeon from Wanganui.

**NZ Saloon Championship** (Stratford Speedway, Feb 20-21): Results confirmed.

**NZ Stockcar Championship** (Woodford Glen, Feb 22): Caleb Ireland made history — back-to-back champion with 82 points, just 1 ahead of Harley Robb (81 pts). Riley Eathorne 3rd (78 pts). Only the 2nd driver to achieve this after Peter Rees (2012-13).

**NZ Solo Championship** (Oreti Park, Feb 23): Congreve won in an epic contest.

**NZ Super Saloon Championship** (Meeanee Speedway, Mar 1): Chris Cowling claimed his 3rd NZ crown.

**ENZED NZ Superstock Teams Championship** (RPIS, Feb 7-8): Gisborne Giants — first title in 10 years since reforming.

**NZ Stockcar Teams Championship** (Rotorua, Mar 7-8): Canterbury Crushers denied the Rascals a three-peat.
