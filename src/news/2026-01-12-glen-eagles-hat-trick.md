# Canterbury Glen Eagles Secure Superstock Teams Hat-Trick

The **Canterbury Glen Eagles** have made history at Palmerston North’s **Robertson Prestige International Speedway** by securing a "hat-trick" of wins at the Superstock Teams Extravaganza.

The final saw the Glen Eagles demonstrate superior blocking cohesion to defeat the **Baypark Busters**.

The meeting was not without controversy, as a semi-final result between the Auckland Allstars and the Glen Eagles was overturned following a disqualification for an Auckland driver "seeking sanctuary" on the infield. Despite the drama, the Glen Eagles' tactical discipline cemented their status as a dominant force in Superstock teams racing.
