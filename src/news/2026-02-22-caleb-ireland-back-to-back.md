# Caleb Ireland Makes History With Back-to-Back NZ Stockcar Titles

**Caleb Ireland** has etched his name into New Zealand speedway history, becoming only the second driver ever to win consecutive NZ Stockcar Championships.

Racing at **Woodford Glen Speedway** in Christchurch on February 22, Ireland finished with 82 points in a nail-biting finale, edging out **Harley Robb** by just a single point (81 pts). **Riley Eathorne** rounded out the podium with 78 points.

The only other driver to achieve back-to-back Stockcar titles was **Peter Rees** in the 2012-13 seasons, making Ireland's feat all the more remarkable.

Registered to **Meeanee Speedway** in Napier, Ireland's consistency across qualifying and finals proved the difference in one of the closest NZ Stockcar Championship contests in recent memory.
