# Brad Lane Claims Historic Fourth New Zealand Modified Title

Brad Lane has further cemented his legacy in New Zealand motorsport by securing his **fourth New Zealand Modified Championship**.

Competing at **Wellington Family Speedway**, Lane navigated a highly competitive field to take home the sport's top honor. The victory adds another chapter to Lane’s decorated career, showcasing the consistency and technical skill required to stay at the top of the Modified class.

The event drew fans from across the region to witness the high-horsepower Modifieds battle for supremacy, with Lane ultimately proving too strong for his rivals.
